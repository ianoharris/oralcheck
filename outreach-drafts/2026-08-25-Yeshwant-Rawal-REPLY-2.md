Hi Dr. Rawal,

Thank you, and thank you for being willing to put your name to it. I want to answer your two questions directly before anything else, because I think the honest answers matter more than favourable ones.

**Is there a formal lab or mentor at UW-Madison?** No. Not yet. OralCheck is an independent project I built on my own, and no UW-Madison faculty member currently supervises it. I am not going to imply otherwise to make this easier. I am actively working to change that, and I have started reaching out to faculty in Otolaryngology-Head and Neck Surgery at UW this week. Your interest is the reason I am now able to make that a serious ask rather than a speculative one, and I will tell you the moment it changes.

**Are there UW-Madison institutional protocols associated with it?** No, and I believe none are required, but I would rather have that in writing than assert it. The reason is that OralCheck collects nothing. The questionnaire is computed entirely in the browser, no answers are transmitted or stored, there is no account, no database, and no record of any individual. Under UW-Madison's Human Research Protection Program, human subjects research requires obtaining data about living individuals through intervention or private information, and none of that happens here. That reads to me as "not human subjects research", but that determination is not mine to make, so I am submitting it to the UW HRPP this week and will forward whatever they return. If your Chair or Associate Dean would prefer that document in hand before deciding, that seems entirely reasonable and I will wait for it.

I should also correct something in my own favour, because I would rather you hear it from me than assume it later.

When you mentioned a design reflecting our two institutions, I want to be clear that OralCheck is not a University of Wisconsin-Madison project. I am a UW-Madison undergraduate, but the university does not sponsor, endorse, review, or have any affiliation with this tool, and I have no authority to put its name or marks on anything. Until that changes, anything co-branded should carry Marquette and OralCheck, not UW. I did not want that ambiguity reaching your Associate Dean through me.

On the systemic factors, thank you for confirming the combined question. I will implement it as we discussed, weighted high, and I will send you the exact wording and weight before it goes live rather than after.

The waiting room flyer is genuinely the piece I am most interested in, and I agree completely on getting through the administrative side first. There is already a printable flyer and a QR generator on the site, and the QR codes can be tagged per location, so a Marquette-specific code would let you see how many people actually scanned it from your waiting area rather than guessing. If that becomes real, I would want your input on the clinical wording well before anything is printed.

The American Academy of Oral and Maxillofacial Pathology possibility is more than I expected from this, and I would be glad to follow wherever you think it should go.

Thank you again. Whatever your Chair and Associate Dean decide, the feedback you have already given has made the tool measurably better and I am grateful for it.
