Hi [Name],

I'm a predental student at UW-Madison, and I built a free oral cancer risk screener called OralCheck (oralcheck.org). I'd like to ask whether it would be useful to your members, and if it isn't, what would make it so.

It takes about two minutes in a phone browser, with no app, no account, and no data stored. Someone gets a risk tier, a plain explanation of which of their own factors drove it, and a push toward a dental exam. It doesn't diagnose. It's available in English and Spanish.

There's no cost and nothing to sign. It's free now and I intend to keep it that way, so this isn't a partnership pitch with a commercial turn later in it.

Two things I'd guess matter to you before anything else does:

(1) The scoring is transparent. Every weight comes from a published odds ratio, with sources listed at oralcheck.org/methods. Nothing is proprietary and nothing is hidden.

(2) Nothing is collected. Answers are computed on the device and never transmitted, so there's no list, no tracking of individuals, and nothing to breach.

If it's useful, there's a printable waiting-room flyer, a QR generator, and a copy-paste embed already built at oralcheck.org/for-clinicians, so it can be handed on to members without any work on your end.

If it's not right for your audience, I'd still value hearing why. I'd rather fix the tool than keep promoting it as it is.
