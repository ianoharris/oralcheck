Hello,

I'm a predental student at UW-Madison and I built a free oral cancer risk tool called OralCheck (oralcheck.org). It asks about risk factors, returns a risk tier, and points the person toward a dental exam. It is free, has no ads and no monetisation of any kind, stores nothing, and is available in English, Spanish and Portuguese.

One page walks through the warning signs with a photograph of each. I have openly licensed images for leukoplakia and for a mixed red and white lesion, but I have not been able to find one for erythroplakia that I am allowed to use, and yours is the clearest example I have seen:

https://exodontia.info/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/Oral-Erythroplakia-1016x688-1.jpg

Would you be willing to let me use that one image on the site? I would credit it as yours with a link back to exodontia.info directly under the photograph, in the same way the other two images are credited to their sources. If you would rather I used a specific wording or a specific link, tell me and I will use exactly that.

I am asking rather than assuming because the whole point of the tool is that a person can check where every number and every claim came from, and quietly using someone else's clinical photograph would sit badly next to that.

If the answer is no, that is completely fine and I will keep looking.

Thank you for the site, incidentally. It has been genuinely useful to me as a student.
